## 1. Agree on definitions

For example, distinguish whether “complete” means implementation finished or also includes user verification. Make the meaning of decision terms explicit.

## 2. Distinguish information and interpretation

Keep “the estimate states eight hours” separate from “delivery will probably be quick.” Record the source and assumptions, leaving unverified points unresolved.

## 3. Separate interpretation outcomes and decisions

Distinguish what has been resolved from what needs more evidence. An interpretation outcome alone does not establish factual truth or permission for an external operation.
